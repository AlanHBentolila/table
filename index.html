Three steps at the counter

You don't change where you eat. You just pay for it ahead of time and keep the difference.

  1. 1

    Buy a pack

    Pick a restaurant in the app and buy 5 or 10 meals, usually 10 to 20% below the menu price.

  2. 2

    Say the line

    When you order, tell the cashier:

    “I have a TABLE meal”
  3. 3

    Scan and save

    Show the code in the app. One meal comes off your pack, and the savings add up on your Savings tab.

Example: 10 bowls at Matsuri are normally $120. The pack is $100, so each bowl costs you $10.

    Built by students who eat at the same five places

    TABLE started as a class project at the University of Miami. We noticed we were paying full price, every week, at restaurants we'd already decided to go to.

    Prepaying for those meals saves members money and gives local restaurants cash before the month starts.
